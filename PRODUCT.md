## Product Purpose

TalentX is an AI-powered Applicant Tracking System. It covers AI job creation, CV screening, candidate ranking, candidate management and a recruitment pipeline. It adds multi-organization access, organization switching, role-based access control, team management, secure authentication, session management, audit logging and organization-level data isolation. This surface is its marketing site; success is a visitor clicking Get Started.
